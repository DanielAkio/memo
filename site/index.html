Sticky memories you pin on top of everything.

Memo is a free, open-source sticky-notes app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Each memory is its own little window, and one click on the pin keeps it on top of every other app.

Download

What it does

  • Pin on top of everything: the to-do list next to the spreadsheet, the command you always forget above the terminal.
  • Quick, formatted writing: checklists, bullets, highlight, text sizes and code blocks.
  • Colours, titles and groups to find and put away memories at a glance.
  • Works offline: everything is saved on your computer.
  • Optional sync with Google Drive: sign in with Google to keep your memories in step across your computers. They are stored in Memo's private folder in your own Drive; there is no Memo server.
